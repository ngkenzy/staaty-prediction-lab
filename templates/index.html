Evaluation protocol

70 / 15 / 15

70% training, 15% validation, and 15% untouched final holdout.

No random shuffle

Events remain chronological so future games and fights do not leak backward.

Shifted histories

Rolling and fighter-history features are calculated from prior events only.

Correct 5-round context

UFC scheduled rounds come from TIME FORMAT, not the actual finishing round.

Champion selection

Candidate models are selected using validation performance before final holdout reporting.

Beyond accuracy

Log loss, Brier score, ROC-AUC, and macro-F1 reveal calibration and class-balance weaknesses.
